01 — Profile

Good people, big problems, the best possible solution.

For more than 25 years I have worked as an electrician and electrical engineer. For the last 15 of them I have specialised in the design and development of surveillance, communications and power systems, with a deep focus on renewable technology and genuinely sustainable solutions.

I have designed celebrated solar, wind and biomass innovations, delivered award-winning security and fire projects, and been part of successful initiatives around the world. What drives me is simple: a passion for working with good people to solve big problems in the best possible way, within the timeline and budget we have been given.

Whether the stakeholder is a customer, colleague, manager, supplier or regulator, I lead from the front. My job is to make sure everyone around me has a safe, productive and collaborative environment, where knowledge is shared, debate is encouraged, and mistakes become lessons in the name of continuous improvement.

I am now looking for a senior or managerial engineering role with an eco-conscious organisation that needs an innovative, people-focused collaborator designing and delivering effective, elegant engineering solutions.
